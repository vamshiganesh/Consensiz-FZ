How It Works

Integrating heavy computation with decentralized integrity.

1. Foundry Fuzzer

Rust-based engine bombards the smart contract with thousands of randomized inputs, seeking broken invariants.

2. IPFS Storage

When a crash occurs, the complete input-to-failure trace is packaged and pinned to IPFS, generating a unique CID.

3. On-Chain Anchor

The CID is logged on Polygon/Ethereum as a tamper-proof audit trail, visible to the public via our dashboard.
