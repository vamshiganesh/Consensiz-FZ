Uniqueness & Future Vision

The "Self-Healing" Loop

Going directly from fuzzing to patching to re-validation is a critical gap identified in 2026 academia. Consensiz-FZ closes this loop, converting an auditing tool into a self-healing security agent.

Temporal Vulnerability Drift

Future integration of Monte Carlo simulations will fuzz against projected future blockchain states (e.g., market crashes). We detect "time-delayed vulnerabilities" before the trigger conditions even exist in the real world.
