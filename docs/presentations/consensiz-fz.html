The Problem & Our Solution

Target Problem Statement

"Bombard a target system with random and malformed inputs, automatically detect crashes and unexpected behaviour, and log the complete input-to-failure chain for analysis."

Our Innovation

Current fuzzers test contracts in isolation, report bugs in terminal text, and leave patching to humans.

Consensiz-FZ is an autonomous pipeline that uses AI to find bugs, visualizes the financial impact live, automatically generates verified code patches, and anchors cryptographic Zero-Knowledge Proofs of the findings.

The Consensiz-FZ Pipeline

1️⃣

Cross-Contract Protocol Fuzzing

We don't just fuzz single functions. We build a dependency graph and inject "chaos" (reordering, timestamp manipulation) across the entire protocol.

2️⃣

Autonomous Auto-Patching

When Foundry detects a crash, an LLM queries a Knowledge Graph to generate a code patch, then automatically re-fuzzes the contract to prove the fix works.

3️⃣

ZK-Proof Tamper Evidence

We run the crash inputs through a RISC Zero zkVM to generate a cryptographic proof of the vulnerability and anchor the CID on-chain. Undeniable evidence.

4️⃣

Live Sankey Visualization

Instead of terminal logs, the fuzzer renders an animated diagram showing exactly how money drains during an exploit, calculating the exact USD impact.

Novelty & Future Scope

Uniqueness

The "Self-Healing" Loop

Going from fuzzing directly to patching and re-validation is an academic gap identified in early 2026. Consensiz-FZ is the first product to close this loop. Combining this with ZK-proofs means we secure contracts and mathematically prove our work.

Future Vision

Temporal Vulnerability Drift

Future iterations will incorporate Monte Carlo simulations to fuzz against projected future blockchain states (e.g., simulated price crashes or liquidity drains).

This will allow Consensiz-FZ to detect "time-delayed vulnerabilities"—bugs that don't exist today, but will trigger when market conditions change.
